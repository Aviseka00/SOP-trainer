Title: Hand hygiene training example
SOP ID: DEMO-HH-001
Revision: DEMO-01
Effective date: Example only — not an approved SOP

1. Purpose
This fictional document demonstrates the application workflow and must not be used as an operational procedure.
2. Preparation
2.1 Check that the designated handwashing station has the approved soap and a clean drying supply.
2.2 WARNING: Do not enter the production area if required hygiene supplies are unavailable. Notify the supervisor.
3. Handwashing
3.1 Wet both hands with water at the designated sink.
3.2 Apply the approved soap to both hands.
3.3 Rub your palms together, then follow the handwashing sequence in the effective site SOP.
3.4 Rinse both hands thoroughly with water.
3.5 Dry hands using the approved drying method.
4. Documentation
4.1 Record completion of the training in the designated training record.
4.2 Do not invent a contact time or acceptance criterion missing from the approved SOP.
